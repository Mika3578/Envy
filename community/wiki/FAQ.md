# FAQ

## Is Envy the same as Shareaza?

No. Envy inherits **some** Shareaza-lineage code (especially Gnutella/G2 paths) and continued through PeerProject before the Envy rebrand. It is not “Shareaza renamed.”

## Is there a stable release on the maintained fork?

Not yet as GA. The fork targets **preview** builds (see README). Do not treat CI artifacts or draft releases as stable unless maintainers say so.

## Where is the truth for protocol support?

[docs/10_dev/status.md](https://github.com/Mika3578/Envy/blob/develop/docs/10_dev/status.md) in the main repository — not this Wiki.

## Is Envy an anonymity tool?

No. Envy is a multi-network file-sharing client, not an anonymity product.

## getenvy.com downloads?

The historical domain may be dead or unrelated to the maintained fork. Build from source or use [Mika3578/Envy Releases](https://github.com/Mika3578/Envy/releases). Avoid unknown mirrors.

## How do I report security issues?

Follow [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/Mika3578/Envy/security/policy). Do not post exploit details or tokens publicly.
