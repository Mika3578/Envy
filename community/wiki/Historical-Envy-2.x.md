# Historical Envy 2.x

**Historical era** — anchored by GetEnvy tag `2.0` (2019-08-01).

## Maintainer announcement (2019)

A Shareaza forum post described Envy 2.0 as the product of roughly two years of development, forked from a PeerProject mirror of Shareaza, and positioned as continued active work after PeerProject “retired” in the maintainer’s wording.

## Documented themes (historical)

- Polished fork direction vs Shareaza r9700 baseline (maintainer comparison)
- Skin and UI work; BitTorrent DHT mentioned in period marketing
- Archives hosted at getenvy.com (see Wayback)
