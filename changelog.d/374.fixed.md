**Kad firewall probe slot leak (#374)** - `CKademlia::SendFirewalledRequest` now allocates the `KADEMLIA2_FIREWALLED_REQ` packet before the outbound firewall check commits a slot, so a packet allocation failure no longer strands a check slot until its TTL expires and delays the Firewalled classification; a refused check releases the packet instead. Duplicate `BeginOutboundCheck` calls for a peer with an outstanding check are covered by a new slot-count regression test.
