**Search filter mask mapping (#432)** - `Settings.Search.FilterMask` is now read and written through one canonical codec (`Envy/SearchFilterMask.h`): bit 4 = hide local files, bit 5 = hide rejected results. `CFilterOptions` previously decoded the two bits in the opposite order from the only writer (`CMatchList::Filter()`), so a mask written by one path read back with Local and Reject swapped on the other. Both paths now share the same encode/decode helpers instead of raw bit positions; persisted settings keep their meaning and no upgrade-time swap occurs. EnvyTests round-trip all 2,048 masks through the production codec, pin the persisted bit positions, and assert the Local/Reject decode mapping directly; the `CFilterOptions` constructor decode is consistency-only because its callers overwrite every field after construction.
