**Upload queue lock contention (#332)** - `CUploadQueues::GetPosition` now reports an explicit state (`qpsReady`, `qpsTransientBusy`, `qpsNoQueue`, `qpsShutdown`) alongside the historical position result. A transient lock wait timeout no longer invalidates a legitimate upload: the ED2K and DC ranking passes defer to their next periodic check instead of closing the client, and `OnQueueKick`/rotation handling no longer sends a premature `FINISHUPLOAD` under contention. Queue deletion and shutdown still fail closed. Contention is counted and logged at most once per 30 seconds with the deferred-lookup volume, distinct from the real "no valid upload queue" rejection message. Documented the Network-before-UploadQueues-before-Transfers lock order with a smoke test (`tests/test_upload_queue_lock_order_smoke.cpp`). Bounded waits (500/250 ms) and shutdown behavior are unchanged; no infinite waits are introduced.
