**Upload queue contention (#332)** - Transient lock contention no longer drops or mis-answers live upload clients: ED2K and DC ranking passes defer to their next periodic pass instead of closing clients, and a contended upload rotation ends the active upload with a finish notice and re-queues the client instead of dropping it. A queue kick still takes effect immediately even when its confirmation lookup is contended, requests keep their existing queue position instead of being re-queued, and repeated contention is summarized in a throttled log message. A deleted queue and application shutdown still fail closed.
