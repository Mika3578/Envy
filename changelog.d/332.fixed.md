**Upload queue contention (#332)** - Transient lock contention no longer drops or mis-answers live upload clients: ED2K and DC ranking passes and upload rotation defer to their next periodic pass instead of closing clients or ending uploads; a queue kick still takes effect immediately even when its confirmation lookup is contended, requests keep their existing queue position instead of being re-queued, and repeated contention is summarized in a throttled log message.
