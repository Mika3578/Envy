**Kad SEARCH_RES hardening (#373)** - `KadParseSearchResBody` now fails closed when the declared entry count does not consume the entire body, so trailing bytes after the last entry reject the frame instead of being ignored. Outstanding search contexts track their registration tick and expire by elapsed subtraction, keeping expiry and eviction correct across `GetTickCount()` wrap; re-registering a target refreshes the context. Cross-target injection was already prevented structurally (contexts are keyed by the packet target hash and delivery additionally requires the peer to have been queried).
