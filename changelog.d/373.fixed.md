**Kad SEARCH_RES hardening (#373)** - `KadParseSearchResBody` now fails closed when the declared entry count does not consume the entire body, so trailing bytes after the last entry reject the frame instead of being ignored. Outstanding search contexts track their registration tick and expire by elapsed subtraction, keeping expiry and eviction correct across `GetTickCount()` wrap; re-registering a target refreshes the context. Cross-target injection is now rejected at delivery: contexts are keyed by the packet target hash, and the responding peer must additionally have an outstanding search request for that same target and search kind.
